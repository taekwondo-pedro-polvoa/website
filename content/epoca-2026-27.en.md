*Porto, 18 August 2026*

**Dear parent or guardian,**

We are delighted to present the **2026/2027 season of Taekwondo Pedro Póvoa**.

This new season starts a new stage, with a project dedicated to **training, development and competition**, aiming to give our athletes the best conditions to learn, improve and reach their goals through taekwondo.

Our priority is to follow each athlete's journey, respecting their age, level of development and goals, promoting technical and physical progress as well as core values such as **discipline, respect, responsibility, focus, confidence and perseverance**.

We want to build a school where taekwondo is much more than a sport: a place for learning, growth and personal development.

## A new place for a new project

From the 2026/2027 season, Taekwondo Pedro Póvoa will train at **Escola Secundária de Fontes Pereira de Melo, in Porto**. This new partnership allows us to develop a sports project open to the community, from first steps to competition.

The school is organised into age groups and learning levels so that training fits each athlete's characteristics and needs. From Bambi to Competition, every athlete has their own path.

More training, more progress, more confidence. During the season there may also be **complementary sessions, training camps, competition training, grading exams and other activities**, according to the school's planning.

## Training and competition

Many years of experience in taekwondo allow us to build a project that combines **sports education** with **high-level competition**. The project is led by **Pedro Póvoa OLY**, a Portuguese Olympic athlete, 7th at the Beijing 2008 Olympic Games, and taekwondo coach.

## A school for athletes and for families

An athlete's development depends on a good relationship between **athlete, family and coach**. We therefore keep close communication with parents and guardians and an environment of respect, responsibility and commitment. Parents' involvement helps follow the young athletes' progress, while our responsibility is to provide guidance, organisation and quality in training.

We hope to count on you in this new stage and to welcome every athlete who wants to learn, improve and live taekwondo with us. **The 2026/2027 season will be the start of a new chapter.**
