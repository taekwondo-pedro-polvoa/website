The 2026/2027 season marks the start of a new stage for Taekwondo Pedro Póvoa: a project dedicated to **training, development and competition**, aiming to give every athlete the best conditions to learn, improve and reach their goals.

Our priority is to follow each athlete's journey, respecting their age, level of development and goals, promoting technical and physical progress along with values such as discipline, respect, responsibility, focus, confidence and perseverance.

## Where we train

From the 2026/2027 season, training takes place at **Escola Secundária de Fontes Pereira de Melo, in Porto**, in a sports project open to the community, from first steps to competition.

## Who leads it

The project is led by **Pedro Póvoa OLY**, a Portuguese Olympic athlete, 7th at the Beijing 2008 Olympic Games, and taekwondo coach. Each athlete can find their own path, whether through recreational practice or through competition and high performance.

*We build champions, we shape people.*

## A school for athletes and for families

An athlete's development depends on a good relationship between **athlete, family and coach**. We keep close communication with parents and guardians and an environment of respect, responsibility and commitment: parents' involvement helps follow the young athletes' progress, and our responsibility is to provide guidance, organisation and quality in training.
