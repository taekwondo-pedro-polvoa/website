Pedro Póvoa is a Portuguese Olympic athlete and taekwondo coach. At the 2008 Olympic Games in Beijing he finished **7th**. Today he is the technical director and head coach of Taekwondo Pedro Póvoa, in Porto.

## From high performance to training

Many years of experience in taekwondo allow him to build a project that combines **sports education** with **high-level competition**. The idea is simple: every athlete should be able to find their own path, whether through recreational practice or through competition and the pursuit of high performance.

## A new school in Porto

From the 2026/2027 season, training takes place at **Escola Secundária de Fontes Pereira de Melo, in Porto**, in a project open to the community, from first steps to competition. There are groups from Bambi to Competition, for athletes from age 4, and the coach places each athlete according to age, experience, technical level and goals.

## Values

More than a sport, taekwondo here is a school for life: discipline, respect, responsibility, focus, confidence and perseverance. The project's motto is *we build champions, we shape people*.
